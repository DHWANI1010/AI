Rules & scoring

Essay rules

  1. Submit your own work, and credit anyone you quote.
  2. Stay within 50,000 characters, as a PDF, Word or TXT file or pasted text.
  3. Write at least 50 words so there is enough to assess.
  4. Name the source for facts, figures and quotes.
  5. Keep opinions marked as opinions, and don't pass hearsay off as fact.

Four criteria, each scored 0 to 5

  • Grammar and mechanics important (30% of the total)
  • Facts vs. rumours important (30%)
  • Individualism: your own voice and ideas (20%)
  • Structure and clarity (20%)

0 = missing, 1 = weak, 2 = needs work, 3 = fair, 4 = strong, 5 = excellent.

How your essay is checked

  • Grammar: spelling slips, repeated words, capitalization, spacing, punctuation and run-on sentences.
  • Facts vs. rumours: sources, dates, figures and citations count for you. Hearsay phrases like "everyone knows" and unsupported absolutes count against you.
  • Individualism: personal voice, word variety, varied sentence openings, and clichés.
  • Structure: paragraphs, sentence length and transition words.
